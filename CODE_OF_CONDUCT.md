# Code of Conduct

QuestBridge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and take feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information aren't acceptable, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project is discussed.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through GitHub's [private reporting](https://github.com/ElasticSea/questbridge/security/advisories/new) form, with "Code of Conduct" in the title. All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
